# Landing Page Brief: Northwind Coffee (fictional sample brand)

## Brand
- Name: Northwind Coffee
- One-line description: Small-batch coffee subscription delivered monthly
- Voice: warm, simple, honest
- Words to avoid: revolutionary, best ever, guaranteed, miracle

## Page goal
- Primary action: Start a subscription
- CTA button text: Start my first box
- CTA link: https://example.com/subscribe

## Audience
- Who they are: Home coffee drinkers tired of stale supermarket beans
- Their biggest problem: Fresh, good coffee is hard to find and easy to forget to buy

## Required sections
- hero
- benefits
- how-it-works
- faq
- cta

## Content rules
- Headline max words: 10
- Benefits: exactly 3
- FAQ: 3 to 5 questions
- No invented statistics, testimonials or awards

## Design reference
- URL or screenshot filename: reference/sample-layout.png (layout only)
- What to copy: single column hero, three card benefits, numbered steps, accordion FAQ
- What NOT to copy: text, logos, images, brand colours

## Colours and fonts
- Primary: #3b2a20
- Accent: #d9822b
- Background: #fbf7f2
- Font: system sans-serif
